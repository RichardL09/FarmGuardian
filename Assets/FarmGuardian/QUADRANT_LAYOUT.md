# Four-section farm layout

The existing `Scenes/FarmGuardian.unity` remains the entry scene. `FarmMapLayout` supplies matching runtime layouts for all four themes; the saved environment preview is regenerated by **Tools > Farm Guardian > Maps > Build Dense Farm In First Scene**.

## Plan (world X / Z, north at top)

```text
                   North village cottages / loop road Z=13.5
       Barn       COWS (-10,7)       Windmill       PIGS (10,7)
                   Upper service apron Z=3.1
       West lane -------- guardian hub -------- East lane
                        Well (-4,-2.8)
                  Fields / small cottage pockets
                  CHICKS (-10,-6)               BUNNIES (10,-6)
                        Lower service lane
                             Player (0,-13)      Exit corridor
```

Pen centres are 20m apart horizontally and 13m vertically, inset at least 10m from the playable boundary. Every pen retains its labelled wooden fence and hinged entrance facing south. Houses form small groups around the north edge and protected interior pockets. The windmill moves to (5,10.5), or (-5,10.5) on Mountain Farm, clear of the four pen entrances. The well remains a separate central landmark; Mountain Farm shifts it to (-7,-2.4) to clear the ridge bypass.

The southeast escape corridor has a divider with northern access, so the nearby lower pen does not give the thief an immediate escape. Advanced Farm mirrors this corridor to the southwest. Woodland has a lower winding approach; Mountain retains two bridge crossings and ridge bypasses; Advanced retains its additional working barn. Roads, animal spawn clearings, plots and entrances are reserved before decoration placement.

## Secured animals

Secured animals keep their state and objective credit while choosing random destinations every 2–4 seconds. Walking speed is 0.45m/s for cows, 0.65 for pigs and 0.75 for chicks/bunnies. Destinations and complete NavMesh paths must stay inside a safe rectangle around the species pen centre (X ±1.1m, Z ±1.35m). A bounds recovery handles avoidance displacement. This restriction also applies while a player or thief opens the entrance. Secured animals are excluded from gate opening sensors; theft and player delivery retain their existing rules.

## Grass and assets

No dedicated grass model was found in the project. `Editor/FarmGrassBuild.cs` creates actual low-poly blade meshes with seven double-sided blades per tuft, deterministic irregular placement and one shared URP material per theme. They are combined into spatial chunks, have no colliders, receive shadows and do not cast them. Road, building, pen and plot clearances remain open. The former low cover made from miniature trees was removed.

Houses, large trees, flowers, fences, storage, crops, barn, well and windmill continue to use the supplied village-town assets and their existing materials. No downloaded replacements or new scene are introduced.
